Click + Exe on your saved grenades (up to 10). Set the player and the time of each one, from the start of the execute.

    How to practice with the .cfg
    1. Download the file and copy it to Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/.
    2. In CS2, open Play → Practice (offline server) on the map.
    3. In the console, type exec pronades_exe_….
    4. Type bind ] exe_next; bind [ exe_prev and use ] and [ to jump between lineups: you land on the pro's exact spot, already aiming.
    5. Offline servers only, never in online matches.
