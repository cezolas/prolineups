How to get your match demo
  1. Premier/MM: in CS2, Watch → Your matches → download the match. The file goes to Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/replays (.dem).
  2. FACEIT: on the match page, Watch demo → downloads a .dem.zst (or .dem.gz). You can load it compressed.
  3. If it comes as .bz2, extract it with 7-Zip first.
