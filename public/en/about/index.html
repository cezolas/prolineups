About Lineups Pro

The lineups on this site are not recorded by hand: they are extracted automatically from the demos (replay files) of professional Counter-Strike 2 matches. For every grenade we record where it was thrown from, the aim, the technique (jump, running, click) and where it landed. Identical grenades thrown by different pros become one lineup.

Where the data comes from

Match demos are downloaded manually from HLTV.org, which keeps the archive of professional matches. Map radars come from the game's own files.
