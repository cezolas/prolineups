Weekly grenade training plan

Pick the map and side you play most. The plan uses the grenades pros use the most, easiest to hardest: 6 days of new grenades and a 7th review day. Tick what you practised (saved in this browser) and download each day's .cfg to practise on an offline server.

How to use the practice pack
  1. Download the file and copy it to Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg/.
  2. In CS2, open Play → Practice (offline server) on the map.
  3. In the console, type exec lineupspro__day1.
  4. Type bind ] lp_next; bind [ lp_prev and use ] and [ to jump between lineups: you land on the pro's exact spot, already aiming.
  5. Offline servers only, never in online matches.
